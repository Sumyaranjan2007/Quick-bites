# Quick Bite Platform -- Privacy Policy (PRIVACY_POLICY)

**Effective Date:** September 5, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026  
**Governing Law:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), Republic of India  

---

## 1. Introduction

Quick Bite Technologies Private Limited ("Quick Bite", "we", "our", or "us") operates the Quick Bite customer mobile application, restaurant partner portal, rider partner application, and administrative web interfaces (collectively, the "Platform"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our customers, merchant partners, and delivery personnel.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

1. **Identity & Contact Data:** Full name, verified email address, verified Indian mobile phone number, delivery addresses, and profile pictures.
2. **Geolocation Data:** Precise GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) captured to enable restaurant discovery, accurate doorstep delivery routing, and live order tracking.
3. **Transactional & Billing Data:** Order histories, item preferences, dietary preferences (such as vegetarian flags), coupon redemption records, and payment transaction identifiers (we do NOT store credit card CVVs or netbanking passwords; all payments are processed via PCI-DSS certified gateways such as Razorpay).
4. **Merchant KYC Data:** 14-digit FSSAI license numbers, Goods and Services Tax Identification Numbers (GSTIN), Permanent Account Numbers (PAN), and bank account verification details for payout settlement.
5. **Technical & Telemetry Data:** Device models, operating system versions, IP addresses, correlation IDs, and crash telemetry.

---

## 3. Legal Grounds & Purpose of Processing

We process personal data solely for lawful purposes under Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023:
- **Order Fulfillment:** Transmitting delivery address and order items to fulfilling restaurants and assigned delivery partners.
- **Safety & Verification:** Verifying order dropoffs via one-time passwords (OTPs) and verifying merchant food safety credentials.
- **Customer Support & Dispute Resolution:** Investigating delivery failures, processing authorized refunds, and responding to consumer inquiries.
- **Fraud Mitigation:** Preventing payment velocity fraud, multi-accounting coupon abuse, and automated scraping.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We do not sell personal data to data brokers. We share data only with verified cloud processors strictly necessary to deliver our services:
- **Supabase Inc.:** User authentication, session management, and encrypted database hosting.
- **Razorpay Software Private Limited:** Payment processing, refund issuance, and fraud tokenization.
- **Firebase / Google Cloud:** Push notifications dispatched to mobile devices.
- **Resend Inc.:** Transactional email delivery and OTP verification dispatches.
- **Cloudflare Inc.:** Edge caching, DDoS protection, and secure image object storage.

---

## 5. User Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023

You have the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:
1. **Right to Access:** Request a complete summary of personal data held about you and processing activities undertaken.
2. **Right to Correction & Erasure:** Request correction of inaccurate information or permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.
3. **Right of Grievance Redressal:** Lodge inquiries or complaints with our designated Data Protection Officer.
4. **Right to Nominate:** Nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

---

## 6. Data Retention & Security

- Personal transaction records are retained for a period of five (5) years to comply with Indian tax regulations and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020.
- Telemetry and session logs are automatically purged after ninety (90) days.
- All stored data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.
