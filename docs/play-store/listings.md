## 2. Quick Bites Partner (restaurants) — `com.quickbite.partner`

**App name** (19/30)
```
Quick Bites Partner
```

**Short description** (76/80)
```
For restaurants on Quick Bites: take orders, manage your menu, see earnings.
```

**Full description**
```
Quick Bites Partner is the app for restaurants that sell on Quick Bites.

Take orders
• New orders ring on your phone
• Accept, set the preparation time and mark the food ready for pickup
• Hand the order over to the rider with a pickup code

Build your menu quickly
• Lay out your whole menu: sections, dishes, prices, sizes, extras and photos
• Take photos of your printed menu and the app fills in the dishes, prices and sizes for you to check
• Mark dishes sold out when you run out
• Every change is checked by the Quick Bites team before customers see it

Know your money
• Earnings after commission, today, this week and this month
• A statement order by order, showing the food total, commission and tax
• Payments to your bank account or UPI ID

Run your kitchen
• Go online or offline with one switch
• Update your restaurant details and opening hours
• Raise a help request and see the replies
• Delete your account from the app

This app is for restaurant owners and their staff. To start selling, create an account in the app; the Quick Bites team checks your licence and details before your restaurant goes live.
```

## 3. Quick Bites Rider (delivery partners) — `com.quickbite.rider`

**App name** (17/30)
```
Quick Bites Rider
```

**Short description** (77/80)
```
Deliver food with Quick Bites. See your pay before you accept every delivery.
```

**Full description**
```
Quick Bites Rider is the app for delivery partners who deliver for Quick Bites.

Choose your work
• Go online when you want to work and offline when you are done
• Each offer shows what you will earn and the trip distance before you accept
• Accept or pass, it is your choice

Deliver
• A map takes you to the restaurant, then to the customer
• Collect the food with a pickup code and hand it over with the customer's delivery code
• Collect cash on delivery orders, or let the customer pay by UPI at the door

Get paid
• Pay is worked out from the road distance of each trip, plus all of the customer's tip
• See today's and this week's earnings, and a statement trip by trip
• Payments to your bank account or UPI ID

Stay safe
• An SOS button on every screen
• Your location is shared only while you are online and on a delivery

This app is for delivery partners. Register in the app and upload your documents; the Quick Bites team checks them before you can take deliveries. Delete your account at any time from your profile.
```
